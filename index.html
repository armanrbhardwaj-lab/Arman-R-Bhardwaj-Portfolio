Operational controls

Operational Review Framework

A reusable review framework for identifying missing entries, stock-transfer mismatches, physical-stock gaps and unresolved wastage postings.

SAMPLE FRAMEWORK
01 · ENTRY COMPLETENESS

Missing or unrecorded entries

Review entry completeness across purchases, purchase returns, sales, production, wastage and physical-stock postings. Classify exceptions by process, outlet, date and owner.

  • Prioritise purchase, purchase return and sale entries
  • Check production, wastage and physical-stock posting
  • Assign owner, due date and closure evidence
02 · STOCK MOVEMENT

Transfer reconciliation

Reconcile stock movement between stores, production, dispatch and outlets, with clear checks for pending receipts and in-transit stock.

  • Match transfer-out with transfer-in
  • Review pending receiving and in-transit stock
  • Reconcile source warehouse, destination and quantity
03 · STOCK CONTROL

Physical stock & wastage

Compare physical stock with book stock and ensure wastage is recorded with a reason, supporting details and approval.

  • Confirm physical stock by location and date
  • Post wastage with reason and approval
  • Compare book stock with physical stock
Recommended analysis workflow

Step 1: standardise review date, client, location, process, status, remarks and owner. Step 2: classify each point as Missing Entry, Transfer Mismatch, Physical Stock Pending, Wastage Pending or Data/Mapping Issue. Step 3: link each exception to item-wise book stock, physical stock and variance quantity/value. Step 4: compare the same item-location pairs at Day 1, Day 30, Day 60 and Day 90. A valid Day 1 vs Day 90 result requires comparable baseline and follow-up quantities and values for the same item-location pairs.
